MeasureHow it's calculated
UPHManifest total units ÷ downstocking labor hours. Downstocking covers OMS orders (break down pallets), manifest validation, and downstock totes and pallets. Unloading is not counted.
Labor hoursEmployees × (end time − start time), added up for every row, minus unpaid full-time lunches.
LunchesFull-time: 40 min unpaid lunch taken out when their total hours across all steps are over 5. It comes out of the steps they worked, by their hours in each. Part-time 15 min breaks are paid, so nothing is taken out.
Double counting checkEach person is entered by first name. If the same person is in two steps at the same time, the app shows who and when, and won't save.
Step ratesManifest totals (pallets, OMS orders, validation units, units) ÷ that step's labor hours.
